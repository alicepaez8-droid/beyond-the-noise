Special Issue & Publication Background

About Salaramin ng Bayan

Salaramin ng Bayan (Beyond the Noise) is an independent digital publication dedicated to examining the economic opportunities, ethical questions, and community impacts surrounding the Pax Silica Initiative in the Philippines.

Logo Design & Identity Explanation

Our official publication emblem features a central shield and truth-reflecting mirror emblem surrounded by laurel leaves. The microchip and water drop icons represent the core tension of the Pax Silica issue: technological innovation versus local utility and environmental sustainability. The crimson and navy palette symbolizes journalistic truth and social impact.
